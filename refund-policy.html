Refund Policy

Transparent and fair refund guidelines.

1. Refund Eligibility

Refund requests are accepted only within 24 hours of payment.

Important Condition

After completing the payment and starting the service, no refund will be initiated.

2. Non-Refundable Cases

No refund will be provided if:

  • Work has already started
  • Design or development process has begun
  • Client delays or stops communication

3. Refund Process

  • Approved refunds will be processed within 5–7 business days
  • Refund will be credited to the original payment method

4. Cancellation

  • Cancellation requests must be made within 24 hours
  • After that, cancellation will not be accepted
